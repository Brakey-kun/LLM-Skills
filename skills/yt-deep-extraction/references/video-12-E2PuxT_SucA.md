## Key Takeaways (Creator's Framing)

1. **Core Thesis**: Stock picking is mathematically inferior to passive indexing due to volatility drag — the arithmetic-to-geometric return gap destroys compounding.
2. **Alpha Illusion**: Most "stock picking alpha" is actually leveraged beta exposure; true idiosyncratic alpha is rare and uncorrelated with market.
3. **Path Dependence**: Investors experience one path, not the ensemble average. Stock picking widens the path distribution, increasing probability of catastrophic outcomes.
4. **Tail Risk Dominance**: In bottom 10% scenarios, stock pickers suffer 80% drawdowns vs. 50% for broad market — survival requires hedge sleeves.
5. **Solution**: Engineer risk exposures (core + hedge sleeve) rather than forecast returns. Monetize volatility, create orthogonal payoffs, smooth the compounding curve.
