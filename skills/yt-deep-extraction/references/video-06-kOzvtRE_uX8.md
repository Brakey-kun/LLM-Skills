## Key Takeaways (Creator's Framing)

1. **CAGR as foundational metric**: Presented as "one of the most important metrics in quantitative finance" for understanding long-term wealth creation through geometric compounding.

2. **Multiplicative vs. linear growth**: Core thesis that true investment growth is multiplicative (geometric), not additive (arithmetic), creating convexity — "growth on growth."

3. **Volatility compression**: CAGR's utility is compressing stochastic, jagged return paths into a single interpretable rate that maps beginning to ending value.

4. **S&P 500 benchmark**: Historical real CAGR ~7.5% → doubles capital every ~9.6 years (Rule of 72). This is the "reality check" against day-trading promises.

5. **Day trading reality check**: CAGR exposes unrealistic claims by showing what sustained compounding actually looks like vs. promised daily returns.
