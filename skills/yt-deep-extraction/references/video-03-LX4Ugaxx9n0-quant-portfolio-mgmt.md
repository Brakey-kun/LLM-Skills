## Key Takeaways [INFERRED]

1. **Foundational thesis**: Investing is not about prediction but survival, positive edge, and time for compounding
2. **Risk framework**: Distinguish diversifiable (idiosyncratic) from market (systematic) risk; only latter commands premium
3. **Volatility drag** is the silent portfolio killer — geometric mean < arithmetic mean by ~σ²/2
4. **Diversification** works in normal times but fails when needed most (crises); seek *physical* decorrelation
5. **Portfolio construction** should start from investor goals/liabilities, not market benchmarks
6. **Hedging** (currency, beta, tail) is a deliberate choice with cost/benefit tradeoffs
