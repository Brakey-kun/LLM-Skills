## Key Takeaways (Creator's Framing)

1. **Volatility drag is the silent killer** of compounding — the gap between arithmetic and geometric returns scales with variance (½σ²).
2. **Stock picking ≠ alpha** — most "alpha" in high-vol strategies is actually levered beta exposure.
3. **Path dependency matters** — you cannot choose your sample path; bottom 10% outcomes for stock pickers are catastrophic (~80% drawdown).
4. **Hedge sleeves are the solution** — engineered convexity/negative correlation allocations monetize volatility and improve geometric returns.
5. **Risk engineering > trading** — shift from prediction to portfolio construction as an engineering discipline.
