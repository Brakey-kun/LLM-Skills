## Key Findings from Available Sources

### 1. Vertical SaaS for Emerging Markets is a Recognized Pattern (HN, 2013-present)
- **pmarca (Marc Andreessen, 2013)**: Vertical SaaS enables "many more categories than traditional enterprise software" and "going downmarket is far easier with the new model" — globalization and developing world could "dramatically increase market size"
- **lifeisstillgood (2013)**: "Find a vertical niche, where specialisation wins... GPS in waste collection lorries... across europe thats a multi billion dollar industry"
- **nostrademons (2019)**: Niche vertical search/software has no business model unless it captures commercial queries (insurance, lawyers, therapists, SaaS, flowers) — the 80% revenue comes from commercial verticals
- **johnrushx (2023)**: Failed by expanding verticals too early (Series A pressure); succeeded by returning to **one product for one niche**, becoming "best in the world for that niche", profitable at ~$1M ARR

### 2. African SaaS Reality (HN Comments)
- **qixxiq (South Africa, 2024)**: B2B SaaS billing site profitable with **local-only users** (<500); team of 1 dev; hiring locally "more than a little challenging"; considering international expansion but worried about support scaling
- **rockwood (2023)**: Pebble (bootstrapped SaaS, profitable) has fully remote team "from Korea to Morocco" — proves Morocco has technical talent employed in global SaaS

### 3. Morocco-Specific Signals (Very Limited)
- **ahmedelalaoui (2025)**: Junior SEO at Moroccan AI startup (larist.ai) — 3 months, flat traffic, 2 weeks to show results or fired. Shows: early-stage AI SaaS exists in Morocco, but marketing/sales capability gap
- **saad-tarhi (2025)**: Ex-Automattic Senior Full-Stack Engineer, **Morocco-based**, looking for remote B2B SaaS roles — confirms senior talent pool exists
- **No English-language discourse** on Morocco vertical SaaS opportunities, market gaps, or success stories

## Recommended Decomposition Strategy

**Source-based decomposition** (not aspect-based) because:
1. Information is scattered across languages and platforms
2. No existing synthesized framework for "Morocco vertical SaaS gaps"
3. Need to mine primary sources (founders, regulators, SMEs) not just secondary analysis

### 6 Source-Based Aspects for Phase 1 Deep Research

1. **Morocco Digital & Regulatory Foundation** — ANRT, Bank Al-Maghrib, OMPIC, CGI, data protection (CNDP), e-invoicing mandate (2026)
2. **Morocco SME Software Reality** — What 95% of SMEs actually use (Excel, WhatsApp, Sage/QuickBooks FR, custom Access/Excel), pain points, willingness to pay
3. **Payment/Billing/Trust Infrastructure** — CMI, wallets, facture.ma, electronic signature, dirham pricing psychology, churn drivers
4. **Transferable Vertical SaaS Models from Comparable Markets** — Egypt, Kenya, Nigeria, SA, Latin America, Southeast Asia — mapped to Morocco vertical analogs
5. **Morocco Tech Talent & Build Capacity** — Developer supply, agency vs. product dynamics, diaspora involvement, no-code/low-code adoption
6. **Concrete Vertical Opportunity Heatmap** — 15-20 specific verticals scored on: TAM, pain intensity, incumbent weakness, payment readiness, regulatory tailwind, talent fit
